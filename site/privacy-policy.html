2. Summary

  • Zingora works offline. You do not need an account, and we do not run a game server.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or other personal details in the App.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The released App will use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show optional rewarded ads and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Ads are not shown during active gameplay.

When ads are requested, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, in order to deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. This processing is carried out by Google as described in:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).

6. Consent

The App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where required, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and to provide privacy choices required by applicable US state laws. Consent is requested before ads are loaded. You can review or change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy & ad preferences. If your device is offline, no ads are requested.

7. Internet access

The game can be played entirely offline. The App uses the internet only to request ads and to manage consent.

8. Children

Zingora is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL TO BE PROVIDED].

9. Data retention and deletion

Game data on your device is kept until you delete it by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google’s policies.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you on our own systems, most requests can be handled on your device as described above. For data processed by Google, see Google’s privacy tools. For any other request, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL TO BE PROVIDED].

11. Security

Saved game data is kept in the App’s private storage on your device and checked for corruption. No method of storage is completely secure, but we design the App to keep as little information as possible.
