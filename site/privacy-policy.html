Privacy Policy

Zingora by Red Pen Studio · Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Who we are

Zingora (the “App”) is an offline-first puzzle game for Android developed by Red Pen Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”). Red Pen Studio is responsible for the App. You can contact us at:

  • Email: mdimroulhassanofficial@gmail.com
  • Postal address: Red Pen Studio, North Jaldi, Post Office: Jaldi - 4390, Banshkhali, Banshkhali Municipality, Chittagong, Bangladesh

2. Summary

  • Zingora is offline-first. Every level, your progress and all game rules work without an internet connection.
  • There is no account and no login. We do not run a game server, and we do not receive your game data.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. We do not track how you play.
  • If you allow it, the App tells Meta (Facebook) when it is installed and opened, so we can measure our Facebook and Instagram app install ads. Nothing about your gameplay is sent to Meta. Where the law requires consent, this happens only if you agree.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or other personal details.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google uses its User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Ad Preferences.
  • Zingora is made for players aged 13 and over.

4. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. We do not operate servers that receive data from the App, and the App contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. The only third-party software in the App that sends data over the internet is Google’s advertising and consent software (sections 5 and 7) and Meta’s install measurement software (section 6).

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to answer you.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads. Google AdMob is the only ad network in the App; there are no other ad networks or mediation partners. Ads appear only:

  • when you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad for an in-game reward; and
  • occasionally as a full-screen ad after you tap Continue on a level’s victory screen. These never appear in the first 12 levels, at app start, during play, or after a lost level.

The App has no banner ads. Ads are never needed to play or to finish a level, and when your device is offline no ads are requested.

When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, in order to deliver and measure ads, to personalise them where you allow it, and to prevent fraud. The App declares the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) for this purpose. Google processes this information as described in:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses cookies and other identifiers in advertising

Our ad requests tell Google that the App is not directed at children, and limit ad content to a maximum rating suitable for teens.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (for example Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).

6. Measuring our app install ads (Meta)

We advertise Zingora on Facebook and Instagram. To learn which of those ads lead to installs, the App includes the core of Meta’s software development kit (the “Meta SDK”, by Meta Platforms, Inc. and Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.). It contains only Meta’s app events part: there is no Facebook login, sharing or Meta ad network in the App, and the App sends no custom events about your gameplay.

What Meta receives: when Meta is allowed (see below), the App’s Meta SDK sends Meta:

  • an event when the App is installed (once) and when it is opened or brought back to the screen, with the time;
  • your device’s advertising ID (and whether you have limited ad tracking), and a random app identifier created by the Meta SDK;
  • your IP address, from which Meta can work out your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, time zone, screen size, carrier, the App’s version and the Google Play install referrer (which ad or link led to the install);
  • technical diagnostics about the Meta SDK itself, if it fails.

Why: only to measure and improve our app install ads (attribution: matching an install to the Facebook or Instagram ad that led to it). Meta processes this information under the Meta Privacy Policy and Meta’s Business Tools Terms.

When it is on: the Meta SDK starts switched off on every device, so nothing is sent to Meta until your consent choice is known.

  • Where consent is required (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Meta is turned on only if you consent in Google’s consent message, including consent for Meta as an ad partner. If you refuse or close the message without choosing, Meta stays off.
  • Elsewhere, Meta is turned on after the App’s consent check. For users in US states with privacy laws, the App always applies Meta’s “Limited Data Use” option, and Meta uses your location to decide where it applies. This limits how Meta may use the data, under Meta’s Limited Data Use terms.
  • If your device is offline the first time you open the App, nothing is sent to Meta until the consent check has run on a later launch.

How to opt out: withdraw or change your consent at any time in Settings > Privacy & Ad Preferences (where a consent choice applies); the Meta SDK then stops sending events and collecting the advertising ID. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings, and manage how Meta uses information from apps in your Facebook or Instagram settings under “Your activity off Meta technologies” (facebook.com/off_facebook_activity).

7. Consent and your ad choices

The App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and to offer the privacy choices required by applicable US state privacy laws. The same choice also decides whether Meta install measurement is on (section 6). The consent check runs before any ad is requested and before anything is sent to Meta. If your device is offline the first time you open the App, the game starts straight away without ads and without Meta, and the consent check runs the next time you open the App while online.

You can review or change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy & Ad Preferences. This option needs an internet connection. If no consent choice applies where you live, the App tells you so.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalised ads are shown only with your consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time in the same place.

8. Internet access and permissions

The game can be played entirely offline. The App uses the internet only to request ads, to manage consent and, when allowed, to send install and app-open events to Meta. It requests the permissions INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to know whether a network is available, so it does not try to load ads while offline), plus the permissions added by Google’s advertising software and the Meta SDK, including the advertising ID permission and Android’s ad attribution permissions. The App may check whether the Facebook app is installed, as part of Meta’s install measurement. The App does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

9. Children

Zingora is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our ad requests and Meta install measurement are not marked as directed at children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact us at mdimroulhassanofficial@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Data retention and deletion

Game data on your device is kept until you delete it. You can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the App or by clearing its storage in your device settings (Settings > Apps > Zingora > Storage > Clear storage). Because we do not hold any of your game data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data processed by Google for advertising is kept according to Google’s policies, and data received by Meta according to Meta’s policies; you can manage it with Google’s and Meta’s privacy tools and your device’s ad settings.

Because we do not hold personal information about you on our own systems, most of these rights are exercised on your device as described above, and through Settings > Privacy & Ad Preferences for advertising. For data processed by Google or Meta, use their privacy tools as described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy. For any other request, contact us at mdimroulhassanofficial@gmail.com.

12. International processing

13. Security

Saved game data is kept in the App’s private storage on your device and checked for corruption. No method of storage is completely secure, but the App is designed to keep as little information as possible.
