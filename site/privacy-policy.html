Privacy Policy

Zingora by Red Pen Studio · Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Who we are

Zingora (the “App”) is an offline-first puzzle game for Android developed by Red Pen Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”). Red Pen Studio is responsible for the App. You can contact us at:

  • Email: mdimroulhassanofficial@gmail.com
  • Postal address: Red Pen Studio, North Jaldi, Post Office: Jaldi - 4390, Banshkhali, Banshkhali Municipality, Chittagong, Bangladesh

2. Summary

  • Zingora is offline-first. Every level, your progress and all game rules work without an internet connection.
  • There is no account and no login. We do not run a game server, and we do not receive your game data.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. We do not track how you play.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or other personal details.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google uses its User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Ad Preferences.
  • Zingora is made for players aged 13 and over.

3. Information stored only on your device

The App stores the following in its private storage on your device so the game works and remembers your progress. None of it is sent to us.

DataPurposeSent to us?
A random installation identifier created by the AppIdentify your local save fileNo
Level progress, scores, in-game currency and items, achievements, daily tasks and Codex entriesSave and restore your progressNo

4. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. We do not operate servers that receive data from the App, and the App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software of ours or of any third party. The only third-party software in the App that sends data over the internet is Google’s advertising and consent software, described in sections 5 and 6.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to answer you.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads. Google AdMob is the only ad network in the App; there are no other ad networks or mediation partners. Ads appear only:

  • when you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad for an in-game reward; and
  • occasionally as a full-screen ad after you tap Continue on a level’s victory screen. These never appear in the first 12 levels, at app start, during play, or after a lost level.

The App has no banner ads. Ads are never needed to play or to finish a level, and when your device is offline no ads are requested.

When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, in order to deliver and measure ads, to personalise them where you allow it, and to prevent fraud. The App declares the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) for this purpose. Google processes this information as described in:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses cookies and other identifiers in advertising

Our ad requests tell Google that the App is not directed at children, and limit ad content to a maximum rating suitable for teens.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (for example Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).

6. Consent and your ad choices

The App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and to offer the privacy choices required by applicable US state privacy laws. The consent check runs before any ad is requested. If your device is offline the first time you open the App, the game starts straight away without ads, and the consent check runs the next time you open the App while online.

You can review or change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy & Ad Preferences. This option needs an internet connection. If no consent choice applies where you live, the App tells you so.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalised ads are shown only with your consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time in the same place.

7. Internet access and permissions

The game can be played entirely offline. The App uses the internet only to request ads and to manage consent. It requests the permissions INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to know whether a network is available, so it does not try to load ads while offline), plus the permissions added by Google’s advertising software, including the advertising ID permission. The App does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

8. Children

Zingora is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact us at mdimroulhassanofficial@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Data retention and deletion

Game data on your device is kept until you delete it. You can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the App or by clearing its storage in your device settings (Settings > Apps > Zingora > Storage > Clear storage). Because we do not hold any of your game data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data processed by Google for advertising is kept according to Google’s policies; you can manage it with Google’s privacy tools and your device’s ad settings.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal information about you on our own systems, most of these rights are exercised on your device as described above, and through Settings > Privacy & Ad Preferences for advertising. For data processed by Google, see Google’s privacy tools. For any other request, contact us at mdimroulhassanofficial@gmail.com.

11. International processing

Red Pen Studio is based in Bangladesh. Google may process advertising data in countries other than your own, under the safeguards described in Google’s privacy policy.

12. Security

Saved game data is kept in the App’s private storage on your device and checked for corruption. No method of storage is completely secure, but the App is designed to keep as little information as possible.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App’s features change. The latest version is always published on this page with its effective date, and the same text is included in the App under Settings > Privacy Policy.
