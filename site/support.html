Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Zingora is fully playable offline. An internet connection is used only for optional ads and privacy choices.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login. Your progress is saved on your device.

How do I keep my progress when I change phones?

Turn on Android backup (Settings > Google > Backup) on your old phone. When you set up the new phone from that backup and reinstall Zingora, Android can restore your saved game.

Will uninstalling delete my progress?

Yes, unless it is restored from an Android backup. Progress is not stored on our servers.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings > Privacy & ad preferences in the App. You can also manage your advertising ID in your device settings.

Are there lives or energy timers?

No. You can retry any level as often as you like.
